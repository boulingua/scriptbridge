How the matching works

Only selected single letters are changed. The percentage applies to eligible letters, not all text. English vowels and ambiguous combinations stay original. Matches are approximate; this is alphabet familiarisation, not translation or pronunciation instruction.

Forms, editable text, code, web addresses, and other languages marked by the page are left alone. Hold Alt on the page to peek at the original. Restoring the original also helps screen readers pronounce the text correctly.

All processing stays on your device. Enabling a site asks for access to that site only.

Learning purpose and alphabet differences

For learning purposes only: these substitutions simplify pronunciation, spelling, stress, dialect and language use. They are not a translation or complete phonetic transcription.

Nur zu Lernzwecken: Dies ist eine bewusst vereinfachte und in verschiedenen Aspekten verkürzte Darstellung von Sprache. Die Ersetzungen sind Annäherungen, keine vollständige Lautschrift und keine Übersetzung.

Cyrillic is used in Russia, Ukraine, Belarus, Bulgaria, Serbia, North Macedonia, Montenegro and by other communities. Russian г and Ukrainian г/ґ differ; Ukrainian and Belarusian use і, Belarusian also ў. Serbian and Macedonian use ј, љ and њ. Bulgarian ъ is a vowel. This option uses only a shared subset.

Nordic is not one alphabet: Danish and Norwegian use æ/ø/å; Swedish uses å/ä/ö. Icelandic æ usually sounds /ai/; þ and ð resemble English th sounds. The practice set uses selected Danish/Norwegian and Swedish matches and does not cover all Icelandic or Nordic letters.
