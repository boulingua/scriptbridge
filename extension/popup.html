How the matching works

Only selected single letters are changed. The percentage applies to eligible letters, not all text. English vowels and ambiguous combinations stay original. Matches are approximate; this is alphabet familiarisation, not translation or pronunciation instruction.

Forms, editable text, code, web addresses, and other languages marked by the page are left alone. Hold Alt on the page to peek at the original. Restoring the original also helps screen readers pronounce the text correctly.

All processing stays on your device. Enabling a site asks for access to that site only.
